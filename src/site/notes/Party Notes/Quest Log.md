[[Party Notes\|Party Notes]]

# Major Quests
## The Long War
- [[Locations/Gorm's Eye\|Gorm's Eye]] is military redoubt and trading post at the edge of the border lands ruled by the [[Factions/Hecate Legion\|Hecate Legion]]
- The [[Factions/Chiropta\|Chiropta]], a rival splinter faction of the [[Lore/Tagmata\|Tagmata]], have been sieging [[Locations/Gorm's Eye\|Gorm's Eye]] for 3 years.
	- The party ventured into the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]] and found the [[Factions/Chiropta\|Chiropta's]] siege camp. 
	- The [[Factions/Chiropta\|Chiropta]] are Chelonians!
- On the 24rd of Vresh (year of 103, Fourth Age), the party returned to [[Locations/Gorm's Eye\|Gorm's Eye]] and found it was under a major attack by the [[Factions/Chiropta\|Chiropta]] and civilians were being evacuated to [[Locations/Atwell\|Atwell]]

## The Kobolds from the North
- On the 14th of Vresh, the party encountered a group of Kobold's lead by [[Mortimus\|Mortimus]], a sorcer.
	- The Kobolds told the party that "a winged beast attacked their home" in the north of this region.
	- The Kobolds were on the way to [[Locations/Gorm's Eye\|Gorm's Eye]] but mentioned possibly going to the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]], where the administration would be more "amenable" to their capitalistic schemes
- In Gorm's Eye, the party discovered that the Kobolds had swindled refugees from [[Locations/Atwell\|Atwell]] of their precious Gems, and they might be looking for secret tunnels to enter the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]]
- As they were leaving the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]] through the cave stairs, the party discovered Kobold footprints in the area where [[NPCs/Monti\|Monti]] had gone missing.
- The Kobold's attacked [[Factions/The Afflicted\|The Afflicted]] with [[Factions/The Dark Lions\|The Dark Lions]]

## A Strange Note
- On the 11th of Vresh, a stranger approached the party on the road and gave them a strange note
	- [[NPCs/Erwin Jest\|Erwin Jest]] told them it was written in Draconic
	- [[NPCs/Brynwulf\|Brynwulf]], friend of [[NPCs/Ruthger Forstronge\|Ruthger Forstronge]] and a priest of [[Lore/Saint Nargo\|Saint Nargo]], believes this note is evidence of a [[Lore/Dragon Cults\|Dragon Cults]] that is trying to summon [[NPCs/Azgomar\|Azgomar]], a cosmic dragon.
- A preacher in [[Locations/The Edge\|The Edge]] was telling people about a new revolutionary community in the north-west of [[Locations/Old Lystria\|Old Lystria]] where a new patron is offering salvation from suffering 
	- The party deduced they were a member of a [[Lore/Dragon Cults\|Dragon Cults]]
- [[NPCs/Brynwulf\|Brynwulf]] told the party to find him in [[Locations/West Heath\|West Heath]]

## Conclave (Starring Ralph Fiennes and Stanley Tucci)
- There is an upcoming [[Lore/Venerated Souls\|Venerated Souls]] conclave in [[Locations/West Heath\|West Heath]] on the 40th of Vresh.

## Haldrin's Tower
- After defeating [[NPCs/Erwin Jest\|Erwin Jest]] and stopping the immediate threat of Wurms, the party found an [[Party Notes/Unsigned Letter to Erwin Jest\|Unsigned Letter to Erwin Jest]] in his possession that mentions [[Locations/Gloomwood Locations/Haldrin's Tower\|Haldrin's Tower]]
	- [[NPCs/Karl Pardo\|Karl Pardo]] told the party that the [[Factions/Masked Order\|Masked Order]] would know more about the Tower
	- A [[Factions/Masked Order\|Masked Order]] mage being tortured by [[Factions/The Afflicted\|The Afflicted]] confirmed the Order had information regarding the Tower, but was executed before they could say more.

## The Afflicted
- **[[Factions/The Afflicted\|The Afflicted]] and their leader, [[NPCs/Jacob\|Jacob]], are trying to destroy [[Locations/The Weeping Eye\|The Weeping Eye]] and the [[Factions/Masked Order\|Masked Order]].** 
- They have a mysterious patron who is assisting them and only communicates with [[NPCs/Jacob\|Jacob]].
	- [[NPCs/Jacob\|Jacob]] was transformed into a sprawling lump of flesh that emanates an eerie purple mist (still is sentient) during the attack by the Kobolds and [[Factions/The Dark Lions\|The Dark Lions]]
- [[NPCs/Jacob\|Jacob]] asked the Party to retrieve [[Krull's Stone of Withering\|Krull's Stone of Withering]] from [[Locations/Gloomwood Locations/The Gloomwood\|The Gloomwood]].
- [[Factions/The Afflicted\|The Afflicted]] appear to be under the influence of [[Lore/Shune the Vile\|Shune the Vile]]

## Heaven Made Machine
- The party found a [[Kytherian Cog\|Kytherian Cog]] in the [[Locations/Citadel of the Scarlet Minotaur\|Citadel of the Scarlet Minotaur]].
- [[Rinariel, the Whispering Blade\|Rinariel, the Whispering Blade]] told the party that there are only 7 [[Kytherian Cog\|Kytherian Cogs]] in the world and that they are connected to [[Lore/Kytheros\|Kytheros]], the God of Time
- [[Hama, Who Counts Sands\|Hama, Who Counts Sands]] told the party:
	- "the cogs fuse with the mechanism to form the device that gives you power from the Lord of Time"
	- Hama stole a [[Kytherian Cog\|Kytherian Cog]] from a temple of [[Lore/Kytheros\|Kytheros]] and appears to be cursed
	- He threw the stolen cog into the cistern of the [[903 - Shattered Tower\|903 - Shattered Tower]]

## Honor Among Thieves
- **The [[Factions/Maazak Ul-Tiur\|Maazak Ul-Tiur]] is seeking [[Kytherian Cog\|Kytherian Cogs]] for an unknown purpose.**
- The Party encountered the [[Factions/Maazak Ul-Tiur\|Maazak Ul-Tiur]] in [[Locations/Atwell\|Atwell]]
	- Using the manure yard ran by [[NPCs/Neeta Oilsweep\|Neeta Oilsweep]] as cover for illicit shipments

- The Party found a letter from [[NPCs/Lesha Aqari\|Lesha Aqari]] addressed to her sister:
	- "I've tracked a [[Kytherian Cog\|Kytherian Cog]] to the halls of Minoros. I may not return, my twin. You know what is it at stake - fear not for me. Hold to the [[Lore/Principia Argentum\|Principia Argentum]], think of mother and father and what they taught us. Find Endrek in [[The Oasis\|The Oasis]]"
	- [[Rinariel, the Whispering Blade\|Rinariel, the Whispering Blade]] told the party that [[The Oasis\|The Oasis]] is located deep in the salt flats of the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]]

## Homecoming
-  [[Factions/The Dark Lions\|The Dark Lions]], a notorious adventuring party, are looking for Paulo. [[NPCs/Cresha Arduinas\|Cresha Arduinas]] may be involved. 
	- The party believes they found their camp in the [[Locations/Caverns of Metamorphosis\|Caverns of Metamorphosis]].
	- [[Factions/The Dark Lions\|The Dark Lions]] attacked [[Factions/The Afflicted\|The Afflicted]] looking for "cog" and "agulhas"

## Hostile Negotiations
- The Party found a secret tunnel to the [[Locations/Djurum\|Djurum]] and encountered the [[Death's Oath\|Death's Oath]], a powerful desert clan.
	- The [[Death's Oath\|Death's Oath]] are allied to the [[Factions/Chiropta\|Chiropta]] and supplying them
- The party found an efreeti's reliquary (lamp) and surrendered it to [[Rinariel, the Whispering Blade\|Rinariel, the Whispering Blade]]
	- [[Rinariel, the Whispering Blade\|Rinariel, the Whispering Blade]] wants to use it to kill a sphinx, [[Uthanax the Unwinder\|Uthanax the Unwinder]]
- The party poisons [[Rinariel, the Whispering Blade\|Rinariel, the Whispering Blade]], who dies on the way to their rendezvous with the [[Factions/Maazak Ul-Tiur\|Maazak Ul-Tiur]].
	- The party fought off Rinariel's cohort and escaped with this body
	- They brand Rinariel's body with the symbol of [[Lore/Saint Terragnis\|Saint Terragnis]] and send the chariot with his body towards the siege camp while making their escape.
