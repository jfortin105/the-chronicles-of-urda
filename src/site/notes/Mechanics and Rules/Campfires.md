#Mechanics-Rules [[Mechanics and Rules/Mechanics - Rules\|Mechanics - Rules]]

## Properties
- Can't be moved once lit
- Lasts 8 hours while at least one character remains near to it
- Casts light out to a near distance

## Creating a campfire
- Option 1: Characters can combine 3 torches into a campfire
- Option 2: Players can scavenge for wood and combine with 1 torch to create campfire
	- DC 12 INT to scavenge
		- [[Mechanics and Rules/Foraging, Hunting, Scavenging\|Foraging, Hunting, Scavenging]]
